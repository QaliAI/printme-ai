## Holiday economics

Printify's [2026 holiday surcharge notice](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/28893325248145-Holiday-Shipping-Surcharge-2026)
specifies $0.40 per US order from October 15, 2026 through January 17, 2027,
regardless of products/providers. The audit date is before the surcharge period.
Live catalog shipping responses did not expose a separate surcharge or selected
shipping method; production order details were not created to inspect it.

Committed shipping tables predate this adjustment. The shared helper reserves
40 cents once at order/bundle level during the inclusive period, and reserves
it conservatively when checking an individual upsell. A provider order quote
already including it must pass alreadyIncluded=true to avoid double-counting.
Tests cover dates, non-US exclusion, quantities, multiple products and included
quotes. No retail prices changed. Before activating offers, reconcile actual
variant costs and selected shipping profile; the live read-only API returned
unknown product costs and multiple US rates, so no guessed values were adopted.

## Image engine and operational limits

Based on current official [image editing](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/images/methods/edit),
[generation](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/images/methods/generate),
and [image generation guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation).
Photo edits default to gpt-image-2.5-sunburst and original concepts to
gpt-image-2.5-flare. Config overrides are OPENAI_IMAGE_EDIT_MODEL and
OPENAI_IMAGE_GENERATION_MODEL. Output is transparent PNG, high quality,
1024x1536, consumed as base64 and persisted in private artwork storage.

Validation checks source type/size/signature, supported models, prompts and
allowed remote storage hosts. Requests have a 90-second overall timeout and
at most one short retry on explicit rate limiting. Quota errors, network
failures, timeouts and 5xx responses are not blindly retried. Logs contain
provider/model/status/request ID/timing, never customer photo or prompt data.
Mock output is blocked in production. Fake transport/storage are restricted
to the existing development-only secret-protected test harness.

The per-instance generation limit uses ephemeral HMAC-derived client keys;
it is not a distributed budget cap. Review account spend/traffic limits before
running acquisition at scale. No distributed limiter or new database was added.
Local color filters are labeled color treatments, not AI transformations.
