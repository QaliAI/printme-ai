## 4. Google Merchant Center Free-Listing Readiness

To capture zero-ad-spend organic shopping traffic from Google Search & Google Images:

1. **Structured Data:** Every product page (`/products/[slug]`) injects Schema.org `Product` JSON-LD:
   - `name`, `description`, `image`, `sku`, `brand`: "PrintMe.ai"
   - `offers`: `price`, `priceCurrency`: "USD", `availability`: "https://schema.org/InStock", `url`
   - `identifier_exists`: `false` (Custom made-to-order goods are exempt from GTIN/UPC requirements).
2. **High-Res Imagery:** White-background primary product photos (`/shop-v2/tee-base.svg`, `poster-base.svg`, `mug-base.svg`) exceeding Google's 800×800 px requirement.
3. **Transparent Policies:** Explicit shipping costs ($3.99 tee, $5.99 poster, $6.39 mug) and reprint/return policy rendered on all canonical product pages.

## 5. Group Order Protocol & Volume Inquiries

For group buyers (families, running clubs, corporate teams ordering 10+ shirts):

- **Inquiry Channel:** Dedicated email alias `groups@printme.ai` routed to merchant support.
- **Volume Pricing Structure:**
  - 1–4 items: Standard retail ($34.00/tee)
  - 5–9 items: 10% volume discount ($30.60/tee, Net Margin ~48%)
  - 10–24 items: 15% volume discount ($28.90/tee, Net Margin ~44%)
  - 25+ items: 20% volume discount ($27.20/tee, Net Margin ~40%)
- **Turnaround Guarantee:** Digital proof preview delivered within 4 business hours. Production submitted to Printify within 1 business day upon invoice payment.
