Clear space & minimum size

XX

X = height of one letter scrap (≈ the “P” scrap). Keep X clear on all sides of the horizontal and stacked lockups. Splatter and halftone may bleed into the clear space; nothing else may.

  • Horizontal: min 140 px wide on screen / 35 mm in print.
  • Stacked: min 88 px wide on screen / 22 mm in print.
  • Below those sizes use the favicon mark (single “P” scrap).
  • Textured (grain filter) only above 320 px; use clean files in UI and email.
  • Mono is one ink (currentColor): pink + yellow scraps become solid ink with knocked-out letters; other scraps become rough outlines. Use for embroidery, labels, one-colour screen print.
  • Never redraw the letters, re-colour individual scraps, or straighten the rotations — the irregularity is fixed artwork.
