# PrintMe.ai / Creative Handoff - v1.0

## Brand proposition
A creative merchandise platform for people who want to make their own thing: upload a photo, cut out the background, transform it using approved AI styles, adapt the artwork to a printable product, and buy it.

## Approved visual reference
`06_References/APPROVED_CREATIVE_REFERENCE.png` is the user's chosen cut-paper visual direction. Preserve its distinct handwritten/photocopied character, contrast, paper layering, and inconsistent baseline. Outlined SVGs are a working reconstruction, not a pixel-perfect approved master.

## Creative territory
Punk gig posters, editorial zines, photocopied flyers, ripped magazine collage, halftone, independent screen printing, DIY sticker culture, lo-fi media. NOT generic AI SaaS, polished tech gradients, simulated e-commerce screenshots, or a grunge texture over a conventional template.

## Two simultaneous experiences
Storefront: loud, art-directed, animated, rebellious. Creation studio + checkout: focused, precise, accessible, predictable.

## Signature scene
A graffiti robot sprays artwork onto a poster wall; torn paper pulls away and becomes a genuine shoppable T-shirt art placement. Robot is a *campaign character*, not a replacement brand mark.

## Reusable motif inventory
1 torn scraps, 2 magazine clippings, 3 imperfect registration, 4 halftone patterns, 5 sticker labels, 6 photocopy noise, 7 tape, 8 paint tags, 9 exaggerated typographic scale, 10 independent original product photography.

## Seasonal system
Core identity remains constant. Halloween can add acid green and horror print motifs. Thanksgiving uses harvest orange with irreverent magazine-style captions. Winter uses holiday red/green sparingly. Each campaign changes drop assets, never the navigation grammar or master logo.

## Motion guardrails
Distinct motion behavior per chapter. Aim for 60fps where possible, test on Android/iPhone, support reduced motion, avoid perpetual scroll hijack, and never obscure an upload or checkout control. Use GSAP / ScrollTrigger for authored sequences if performance warrants.

## User-facing promises
Creative freedom is not literally printing on *anything*: respect approved catalog, fulfillment rules, safe zones, provider restrictions, IP limits, and print file quality. Do not overclaim sales data or imply demo assets are genuine orders.

## Review gates
Gate 1 brand marks approved; gate 2 hero + graffiti motion prototype; gate 3 product art and mockups proofed; gate 4 create/print flow functional; gate 5 all user-critical screens/mobile/accessibility QA passed.
