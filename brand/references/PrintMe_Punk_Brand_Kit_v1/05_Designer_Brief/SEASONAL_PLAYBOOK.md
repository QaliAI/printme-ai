# Persistent punk identity + seasonal drop skins

Core black / newsprint / pink / yellow remains. All campaign art is its own asset collection with controlled publication dates and approvals. Do not seasonally recolor the logo.

Halloween: acid green + horror red; photocopied B-movie sheets, VHS noise, original monster humor. Respect rights of existing horror franchises. Hero 'THE SLASHER SHOP'.

Thanksgiving: harvest orange + cider tones; absurd family table newspaper collages, sarcastic apparel. Hero 'THE FAMILY SURVIVAL ISSUE'.

Christmas: red/green + silver where suitable; DIY holiday flyers, gift-tag paper scraps, funny original characters. Hero 'HOLIDAY ANARCHY'.

Other drops: Valentine's, graduation, pet month, back to school, summer. Campaign pages contain real inventory, start/end times, approved imagery, alternate stills, archive behavior and ownership metadata.
