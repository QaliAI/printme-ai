# Voice, writing and tone

## Personality
Defiant, playful, creative, sharp. Not mean-spirited, not cynical toward customers, not fake underground posturing.

## Lines / hierarchy
Campaign (edgy): MAKE SHIT. PRINT SHIT.
Universal: YOUR IMAGINATION. NO PERMISSION REQUIRED.
Homepage proposition: TURN YOUR IDEA INTO SOMETHING REAL.
Primary CTAs: MAKE SOMETHING / SHOP THE DROPS.
Utility CTA: UPLOAD A PHOTO.
Checkout: PAY SECURELY (avoid creative obscurity).

## Sample lines
- YOUR PHOTO. THEIR DOUBLE TAKE.
- NOT OFF THE RACK. OUT OF YOUR HEAD.
- MAKE IT WEIRD. MAKE IT YOURS.
- PRINT THE THING YOU CAN'T FIND ANYWHERE.
- THE INTERNET IS LOUD. WEAR SOMETHING LOUDER.

## Avoid
'Unlock your creativity with the power of AI', false scarcity, claims that all imaginable products are printable, fabricated bestseller numbers, unnecessarily profane error messages, and slang that dates quickly.

## Accessible/product writing
Write instructions literally in the creation studio: 'Upload a photo' not 'Feed the machine'. Explain print-safe area, resolution, and estimated delivery in plain language. Friendly error copy that tells customers what to do next.
